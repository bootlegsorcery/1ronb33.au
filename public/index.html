A blog following the culinary adventures of 1RON and B33 from the Material Plane to the Astral Sea.

Who Are You?

Welcome, weary traveller. We are 1RON and B33 — two culinary explorers charting a course across the planes in search of legendary flavors. From the smoky taverns of the material plane to the ethereal banquet halls of the astral sea, every meal tells a story. This compendium is our journal: a collection of recipes and reviews from our adventures. Pull up a chair, pour yourself a flagon, and join us.
