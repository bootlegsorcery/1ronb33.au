Rem Sleep

Penned on January 11, 2026

Ingredients

  • 1 ounce vodka
  • 1 ounce coffee liqueur
  • ½ ounce blue curaçao
  • 1 ounce heavy cream

Instructions

  1. Fill a cocktail shaker with ice.
  2. Pour in the vodka, coffee liqueur, and blue curaçao.
  3. Shake well and strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
  4. Slowly pour the heavy cream over the back of a spoon to float it on top.
  5. Serve without stirring.
