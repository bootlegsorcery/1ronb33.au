Kincardine

Penned on January 11, 2026

Ingredients

  • 2 ounces rye whiskey
  • 1 ounce sweet vermouth
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters

Instructions

  1. Fill a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Pour in the rye whiskey, sweet vermouth, and Angostura bitters.
  3. Stir well until chilled, about 30 seconds.
  4. Strain into a chilled coupe or cocktail glass.
  5. Garnish with a maraschino cherry or lemon twist, if desired.
