## Ingredients

- Ice (2 ounces)
- Vodka (1 ounce)
- Grenadine (1/2 ounce)
- Freshly squeezed lime or lemon juice (optional) (4 ounces)
- Lemon-lime soda
- 1 maraschino cherry

## Instructions

1. Fill a glass with ice.
2. Pour in the vodka and grenadine.
3. If using, squeeze in the fresh lime or lemon juice.
4. Top with lemon-lime soda and stir gently.
5. Garnish with a maraschino cherry.
