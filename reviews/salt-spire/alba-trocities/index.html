Drink Rating Notes
Fowl Play 4 / 5 Served in a crystal glass etched with ancient runes—the bartender claimed they were purely decorative, but my arcane sensors registered a faint thaumic signature. The spirit opens with floral notes, then unfolds through layers of saffron, cardamom, and something I can only classify as “forbidden wisdom.” It is not a casual drink. It demands contemplation. I spent seventeen minutes in silence after the first sip, contemplating the nature of consciousness. This is drinking for philosophers.
The Pecking Order 5 / 5 A savory cocktail featuring spiced spirits and roasted herbs, garnished with a tiny feather. The flavor is bold and unconventional—smoky, herbal, and faintly meaty in a way that should not work but absolutely does. The feather appears to be from a griffon, though I could not confirm this. I suspect the drink changes subtly depending on where one sits in the tavern. The bartender only smiled when I asked.
Talon-tini 5 / 5 Sometimes brevity is the highest form of praise. A classic martini executed with absolute precision—crystal-clear, ice-cold, garnished with a single olive positioned at exactly seventeen degrees from vertical. I measured. The gin-to-vermouth ratio is perfect. I have nothing to add. Nice.
