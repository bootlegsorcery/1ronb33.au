Drink Rating Notes
Featherfall 3 / 5 Upon consumption, I experienced a brief alteration to my mass-to-gravity ratio—approximately four seconds of genuine weightlessness. The bartender advised this effect normally lasts minutes for organic beings. Something about my metallic composition interferes with the enchantment. The drink itself is light and effervescent, tasting of pear and white tea. The gimmick is impressive engineering, but the duration makes it more of a party trick than an experience.
Darkroost Stout 5 / 5 A proper stout. Thick as oil, dark as a moonless night, with a creamy head that lingers for a full minute after pouring. The malt profile is roasted to the edge of bitterness without crossing over. Nitrogenated for texture. I have limited reference for “what the Irish would think” but my data suggests this would meet their specifications. B33 called it “a religious experience.”
