Drink Rating Notes
Rem Sleep 5 / 5 A layered cocktail of vodka, coffee liqueur, blue curaçao, and heavy cream. Served at cruising altitude aboard the Sky Caravan, where the clouds drift past the windows like slow-motion smoke. The cream floats on top in a perfect layer—the bartender achieved this through technique, not magic, which I respect. Smooth, calming, and soporific. I did not sleep (I cannot) but my systems entered a maintenance cycle approximating deep rest.
