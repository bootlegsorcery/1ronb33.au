Drink Rating Notes
Screetch on the Beach 3 / 5 A coconut rum drink served in a tall glass with a paper umbrella. A local described it as “hard to take, like my situationship” and I have logged this phrase for further linguistic analysis. The drink is indeed challenging—sweet to the point of aggression, then abruptly sour. I do not know what a situationship is, but I suspect it is unpleasant.
Whi-skreeeeee Sour 5 / 5 Best in the business. The claim is accurate. A whiskey sour made with what I believe was a single-barrel rye, fresh lemon, and egg white shaken until my arms would have ached had I been organic. The foam stands two fingers high and holds for minutes. I returned the following evening for a second. It was equally perfect. Consistency at this level is rare.
Sweet Marsckreeni 3 / 5 A vibrant ruby blend with floating slices of enchanted citrus that glow faintly. The first sip dances across the tongue with bright berries and spice before finishing with a flourish of feywild sweetness. Several patrons reported an irresistible urge to twirl after consuming it. My gyroscopic stabilizers registered no such compulsion, but I observed the effect in others with scientific interest. Whether magical or merely festive, I cannot say.
Sanskreeta 5 / 5 An astonishingly smooth dessert cocktail made from honeyed cream liqueur and rare moon-orchard fruits. Rich without becoming cloying—it tastes like a halfling grandmother’s secret recipe elevated by arcane intervention. Dangerous only because you will want another immediately. I understand now why the bat-folk of Bat-tle Cry speak of this drink in hushed, reverent tones.
