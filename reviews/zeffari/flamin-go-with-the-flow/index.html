Flamin-go with the Flow

Penned on January 11, 2026

Drink Rating Notes
Pink Mirage 3 / 5 A pale pink gin-based drink that looks far more promising than it tastes. The flavor is thin—gin and soda with a whisper of berry. The name suggests an oasis, but the reality is more of a puddle. I added lemonade from a nearby pitcher and the resulting mixture scored a full point higher. The bartender should consider this feedback.
Sashaying Sangria 4 / 5 I have traversed roads both royal and muddy, and finally found myself seated at Flamin-go with the Flow with a flight of their most celebrated concoctions. This sangria is the standout—a confident blend of wine, brandy, and tropical fruit. Some were delightful. Some were dangerous. A few may have briefly altered my relationship with gravity. The sangria was the anchor that kept me grounded.
Oasis Elixir 4 / 5 A foundational drink from which many local recipes spawn—gin, coconut water, lime, and a proprietary spice blend the bartender refused to disclose. I attempted spectral analysis but the results were inconclusive. Cannot fault the classic. It is balanced, refreshing, and exactly what one wants after crossing the Zeffari dunes.
Flamingo’s Flambé 5 / 5 Mango juice, rum, and a flash of flame that caramelizes the rim. It is, as B33 described, “adult mango juice”—though I would add that the caramelization introduces complexity that elevates it beyond mere fruit cocktail. I requested the bartender flambé mine twice. She complied. I now understand why the flamingo is the mascot of this establishment.
Long-Legged Lemonade 3 / 5 Vodka, lemonade, and a sprig of something green I could not identify. A local at the bar described it as “yeah it’s alright” and I can find no fault with this assessment. It is precisely alright—no more, no less. I recorded it as the mathematical midpoint of the rating scale.
